Spoiler Alert!

The winning strategy is similar to that of Peres-1:

Pick one out of each pair of outer circles in line with a pair of inner circles and supplement them with the inner circles that may be needed.

You can pick the outer circles in 2^10 = 1024 ways and in some cases this will result in a win, but if it doesn’t it should be obvious which inner circles to pick (just look at the scoreboard!)
